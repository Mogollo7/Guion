# Promo storyboard (16:9, 30 fps, 120 BPM: 1 beat = 15 f, 1 bar = 60 f)

| # | Scene | Bars | What happens | Copy (max) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook | 2 | a question or a striking real image; the mascot peeks in | 3–4 words |
| 2 | Mascot / brand voice (optional) | 2 | the mascot jumps and waves; a speech bubble introduces the product | 2 bubbles × 6 words |
| 3 | Logo | 2 | the logo builds (stroke → fill → name), a burst on the downbeat; the tagline in kinetic type | tagline ≤ 7 words |
| 4–8 | Feature beats ×3–5 | 2–4 each | device enters (spring, tilt) → one interaction (tap / drag / arc) → the real UI piece pops out → result moment (counter, check, confetti) | step tag + 1–3 words |
| 9 | Payoff / outro | 3 | the promise in 3 kinetic lines; logo + URL; mascot waves; credits in small print | ≤ 8 words |

Total: 45–60 s for a full promo, 15–30 s for an app-store-style preview (keep only hook, 2–3 features and the logo).

## Layout per feature scene
- Left third: step tag (number + label) and a kinetic headline.
- Centre or right: the device, tilted 6–14°, floating ±6 px on a slow sine. Alternate sides between scenes.
- A big accent circle or blob behind the device in the brand's accent colour, as in Mixkit's "Smartphone Promo"
  template.
- Exploded UI pieces around the device, linked by dashed lines to where they came from, with an uppercase label above
  each.
- Mascot in a free corner, 400–520 px tall, looking at the action.
- A small "illustrative" badge near anything simulated.

## Copy rules
- Verbs first: "Sube una foto", "La IA identifica", "Directo al mapa", "Expertos validan".
- Use the product's own words (its headings and CTAs), not generic SaaS language.
- One highlight per headline (marker swipe behind the key word).

## Transition kit
- Iris (circle) in the next scene's background colour, from the point where the next action happens, over 12 frames.
- Or a dip to a brand colour over 6–10 frames. Avoid crossfading two busy layouts: it makes a muddy double exposure.

## Audio
- Music track at 108–136 BPM, starting at frame 0 (offset the file so its first strong beat is at 0).
- 1 accent per key action: tap/click, drop, card pop, check (bong/ding), logo (impact), whoosh on transitions.
- Mix target for web: about −14 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP.
